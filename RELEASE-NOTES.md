# Infinity Blade Trilogy for PS5 1.0

All three Infinity Blade games on the PS5, at 60 fps with full controller support.

## Features

- **Infinity Blade, Infinity Blade II and Infinity Blade III**, each as its own PS5 app
- **60 fps** in all three
- **Controller support** for menus, fights, magic and cutscenes
- **Movies and sound** as in the original games, with loading screens that keep playing while the game loads
- **Fast install**: each game unpacks from your own `.ipa` in under a minute; IB2 and IB3 read their big
  texture files straight from the `.ipa` (about 1.5 GB less on the disk for IB3)
- **Quicker loading**: textures are converted for the PS5 in native code and kept for the next time
- **Infinity Blade 1** on the ib-2-3-android port for the first time: OpenAL sound, its movies, its title
  screen, full-size weapon and armour textures and 8× texture filtering

## Installing

1. Unzip the app and copy its folder to `/data/homebrew/` on the PS5.
2. Put your own `.ipa` of the game in `/data/homebrew/<app>/game`.
3. Start the game.

The apps contain no game files.
